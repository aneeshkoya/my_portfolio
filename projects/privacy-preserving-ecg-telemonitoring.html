Case Study

Privacy-Preserving ECG Telemonitoring

PhD research on secure and energy-efficient ECG telemonitoring in Wireless Body Area Networks.

WBANIoTECGInformation SecurityRaspberry PiOdroid-XU4

Technical approach

Signal compression, machine-learning-based detection, authentication and key agreement, secret sharing and privacy-preserving outsourcing.

Constraints

Protect sensitive ECG data while respecting energy, computation and communication constraints of body-area networks.

Implementation

Research prototypes were implemented using Raspberry Pi and Odroid-XU4 platforms.

Outcome

Completed PhD research at NIT Calicut and contributed to SCI-indexed publication record.
