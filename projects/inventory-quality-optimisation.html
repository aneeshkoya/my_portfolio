Case Study

Inventory Quality Optimisation

Automation for identifying irrelevant domains and apps, improving Elasticsearch matching thresholds, managing exclusions and evaluating contextual relevance.

ElasticsearchMonitoringQuality controlOperational intelligence

Problem

Irrelevant domains and apps can reduce contextual relevance and campaign quality unless monitored and excluded systematically.

Approach

Automated quality monitoring, domain and app analysis, threshold tuning, exclusion management and relevance evaluation.

Operational ownership

Connected technical thresholds to campaign-level visibility and practical quality controls.

Results

Supported filtering of 10K+ low-quality domains and apps monthly and monitoring across 100+ active campaigns.
